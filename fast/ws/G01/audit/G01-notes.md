## Judgment calls and overlaps (for the importing tasks)

- Overlap avoided: rp1r04 already asks where the left gonadal vein drains, so rp20 has no rapid item on that; the left versus right varicocele reasoning sits in rp20q05 (a left varicocele that does not empty when supine, with hematuria and erythrocytosis) and the topic's own pretest and self-explanation. rp21r01 (BPH zone), rp21r02 (DHT), rp7r06 (estrogen closes epiphyses), rp22r02 (inhibin B) and rp22r09 (3-month clock) already exist, so rp19 asks about the peripheral zone, aromatase, the 50 to 100 times intratubular gradient and the fever-then-repeat-semen-analysis case instead of repeating them. rp5q11 covers the cancer risk after orchiopexy as a risk question; rp20q10 asks for the intratubular precursor lesion (GCNIS) and carries the timing and contralateral-testis points in its explanation.
- rp19q08 and rp19q09 are deliberately paired (hCG acts on Leydig cells, then FSH is added for Sertoli cells) so that the same four germ-line and stromal cell distractors recur and Weak Spots can name the confusion. rp20q07, rp20q09, rp20q13 and rp20q14 reuse the same tumor-name distractors for the same reason.
