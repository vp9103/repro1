## P2.V F35 fix (reopened 2026-09-27)

The second phase-2 verification failed parity row F35 (global search, K19). Every layer was indexed and a clicked
result opened the right item, but the verifier found two defects:
1. `hi()` highlighted over HTML it had already marked up, which garbled result titles.
2. Scoring was an OR of substring counts, so short words set the ranking.

Only search changed. The edit is confined to the `engine.search` region of `engine/engine.js`, from
`let SEARCH_INDEX` to `hi()`. `openSearch`, `closeSearch` and `searchGo` are unchanged, and so is every line outside
the region (diffed against the pre-fix copy). `shell.html`, `selftest.js`, `engine/base/` and `extract_engine.py` are
untouched.

**Causes** (reproduced first with the old engine on the stub at 1280, light)
- **Garbling.** `hi()` ran one `replace` per term over its own output. A later term matched inside the `<mark>` tags
  it had just inserted, and inside the `&mdash;` entity in every subtitle.
  - "time to select a dominant follicle" rendered "Which cells' response ark>toark> the drug" and a literal
    "&md a sh;".
  - The DOM probe counted 16 `.st1`/`.st2` blocks holding stray markup, and 48 pieces of literal entity or "ark>" text.
- **Ranking.** The same query put the thyroid topic (t:en5) on top, and Enter opened it. rp8q01, the item that
  holds the phrase, ranked 4th. For "no ovulatory role", rp8r01 ranked 12th of 20.
- **The fixed kind order hid the top hit.** Results were shown under a fixed kind order (Topic, Question, Rapid
  review, and so on), so even a correct top hit of another kind was never the first button.

**Fix**
- **Index.**
  - Each piece of an entry is normalized once: tags stripped, entities decoded, lower case, "&" read as "and", and
    every run of non-letters one space.
  - Each piece is filed under a weight:

    | Weight | Pieces |
    |---|---|
    | 5 | title |
    | 3 | bottom line, `pt.say`, image dx/look, figure caption, topic heading/subtitle |
    | 2 | per-option why (q.w, r.w, image ww), table cell (q.et, topic tables), topic why/call-out, figure teach, overlay label |
    | 1 | stem, explanation, options, prose, steps, svg text |

  - `e.N` holds one string per weight, with its pieces joined by "|", so a phrase can never run from one piece into
    the next.
  - `title` and `body` keep exactly their old raw form, because the probe reads them. `tt` and `sub` are plain
    display text; `sub` no longer carries `&mdash;`.
  - The indexed content is the same set as before.
- **Query.**
  - The query is split the same way.
  - Stop words and 1–2 character tokens never make a match or add to the score. They stay in the phrase.
  - A query made only of short tokens ("LH", "T3 & T4") uses them as whole words, so it still finds things.
  - A query with no usable word ("a*[d]? to", "&&") says so instead of listing noise.
- **Rank.**
  - Tier 3: the whole query appears as a phrase (only for queries with 2 or more non-stop tokens). Ties go to the
    heavier piece.
  - Tier 2: every word appears. Scoring terms can be anywhere; short tokens must be whole words.
  - Tier 1: some of the words appear.
  - Inside a tier, the score is the sum over terms of idf (rarity over the index) × the heaviest weight the term
    lands in. A hit at a word start counts 1; a hit inside a word counts 0.5, and only for terms of 4+ letters, so
    "thyroid" still finds hypothyroidism.
  - Entry length and repeat counts do not score.
  - The 40-result cap is applied after this sort.
- **Display.**
  - Groups are (tier, kind), in the order of their best hit. The first button, which is pre-selected and is what
    Enter opens, is the top-ranked result.
  - No weaker hit of one kind sits above a phrase hit of another kind.
  - Tier names ("Exact phrase", "All words", "Some words") appear only when more than one tier is listed.
  - `res.scrollTop` resets to 0 on each search.
- **Highlighting.**
  - `hi(s, Q)` takes plain text and cuts it into words once (letters and digits; "&" reads as "and").
  - It marks by position: the phrase first, then the terms, longest first. Terms are marked the way the ranker
    matches them: at a word start, inside a word for 4+ letters, whole words for short tokens. Stop words are never
    marked alone.
  - It escapes every piece afterwards, so a mark cannot open inside a tag or an entity. No query text ever becomes a
    pattern, so parentheses, `*`, `[`, `?`, `$` and `&` are inert.
  - Why this is not "one combined regex": I tried a combined regex first. Its word-start rule needed a leading
    lookbehind, and a lookbehind in front of a long phrase made each new per-keystroke regex cost 6–21 ms to warm up.
    The word-cut approach has the same single-pass, longest-first semantics.
- **Speed.**
  - Per-term results (heaviest weight per entry, and document frequency) are cached on the index. Each keystroke
    therefore scans only the word being typed.
  - Measured at 1280 on the 48-topic copy described below (350 entries):
    - index build: 44 ms, once;
    - typing "time to select a dominant follicle" key by key: 202 ms over 34 keystrokes, at most 9 ms per keystroke;
    - the old engine on the same page: 301 ms over the same keystrokes, at most 16 ms.
