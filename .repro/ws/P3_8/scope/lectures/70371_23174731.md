## Slide 4: Master gland

- The pituitary gland, controls many of the hormones that circulate in our bodies
- The pituitary gland is roughly the size of a pea and projects out anteroinferiorly from the hypothalamus.
- The pituitary is located just inferior to the optic chiasm (where the optic nerves cross) and sits in the sellar space within the sella turcica (Turkish saddle), a saddle-shaped depression in the sphenoid bone of the skull.
- The pituitary consists of two distinct lobes that project from the hypothalamus by a thin stalk called the infundibulum

## Slide 5

- The anterior pituitary gland, also known as the adenohypophysis, is formed from the Rathke pouch, a finger of ectoderm that buds off from the roof of the developing mouth. In contrast, the posterior pituitary gland, also known as the neurohypophysis, is made of nerve axons that extend from the hypothalamus. At the same time the Rathke pouch is developing, another pouch of neuroectoderm extends from the developing brain to become the posterior pituitary.
- The two adjoining tissues form the mature pituitary gland. However, their histology is different.

## Slide 6

- The two lobes are the anterior pituitary (adenohypophysis) and the posterior pituitary (neurohypophysis).
- The anterior pituitary is a true gland, with cells that synthesize and release hormones. It is richly perfused with capillaries.
- The posterior pituitary is a neural extension of the brain. It is connected to the hypothalamus through the infundibulum, which contains the hypothalamo-hypophyseal portal tract
- The tract carry oxytocin and antidiuretic hormone (ADH, vasopressin) from the nerve cell bodies in the hypothalamus, where they are made, to the nerve endings in the posterior pituitary, where they are stored

## Slide 33: Indications for surgery

- Visual Impairment:
- Surgery is a primary treatment for adenomas that are pressing on the optic chiasm or optic nerves, causing visual field defects or other visual abnormalities.
- Pituitary Apoplexy:
- If the pituitary gland experiences a sudden hemorrhage (pituitary apoplexy), surgery may be needed to relieve pressure on the brain and potentially improve vision.
- Secreting Adenomas:-- except for prolactinoma
- Surgery is often the first-line treatment for secreting adenomas, such as those causing acromegaly or Cushing's disease, when other treatments like medication have not been effective.
- Large or Invasive Tumors:
- Tumor Growth:
- Persistent Symptoms:-If headaches, facial pain, or other symptoms persist despite other treatments, surgery may be an option.
- Failure of Other Treatments: -If medical treatments (like medication or radiation) are not successful in controlling the adenoma or its symptoms, surgery may be considered

## Slide 34: Treatment

- Medical stabilization:
- This includes administering Pre/ post operative high dose corticosteroids, monitoring fluid and electrolyte balance and pituitary hormonal defects post op
- Surgical decompression: surgical removal of the tumor or decompression needed to improve vision
- Prognosis:
- Early detection and treatment are crucial for a positive outcome.
- Visual symptoms may improve with medications or surgery, with full recovery occurring in about 40% of cases.
- About 80% of individuals require long-term hormone replacement therapy.

## Slide 37

- Trans-sphenoidal hypophysectomy - procedure most commonly used to remove a pituitary gland tumor. Trans-sphenoidal means through the sphenoid sinus – the air sinus cavity at the back of the nose.
- The pituitary gland is within the sella turcica or the hypophyseal fossa. This structure is present near the center at the base of the cranium. The anatomical boundaries of the gland have clinical and surgical significance. Sella turcica is a concave indentation in the sphenoid bone. The reflections of the dura bound the fossa laterally and superiorly.The diaphragma sellae is a dural fold with a central aperture, and it covers the sella turcica as a roof incompletely. The adenohypophysis is separated from the optic chiasm by the diaphragma. It is continuous with the dura. The pituitary stalk and the blood vessels travel via the central aperture.he lateral walls of the pituitary fossa are made up of dura mater, and it contains the cavernous sinus. The cavernous sinus consists of the internal carotid artery, sympathetic fibers, cranial nerves III, IV, V, and VI.

## Slide 47: Testing – Water deprivation Test

- The physiological basis for the WDT is that dehydration causes an elevation in plasma sodium concentration, which stimulates AVP release. AVP is carried from the circulation to the kidneys, where it binds to V2 receptors, causing elevation in urine osmolality. If the stimulus to AVP secretion is sufficient (plasma osmolality >295 mOsm/kg), urine osmolality should rise to over 700 mOsm/kg.
- P.Osmolality (mOsm/kg) =
- 2[Na+] + [Glucose]/18 + [BUN]/2.8

## Slide 59: Pituitary apoplexy/ infarction

- Medical and surgical emergency – ACUTE condition where the pituitary gland experiences a hemorrhage or infarction (tissue death). This usually occurs within a pre-existing pituitary tumor, often an adenoma. This requires prompt diagnosis and treatment to improve outcomes.
- Causes:
- Bleeding into and ischemia of the pituitary
- Risk factors:
- These include MACROADENOMA , prior radiation, pregnancy (Sheehan syndrome), trauma, surgery, and anticoagulation therapy.

## Slide 60

- Symptoms:
- Sudden and severe headache.
- Visual disturbances (visual loss, double vision).
- Nausea, vomiting.
- Altered mental status.
- Signs of meningeal irritation (stiff neck).
- Hormone insufficiency.
- Ophthalmoplegia (paralysis of eye muscles).
- Diagnosis:
- Imaging: CT scans and MRI are used to confirm the presence of hemorrhage or infarction in the pituitary gland.
- Blood tests: Evaluate hormone levels and electrolytes
- Immediate treatment with steroids ( don't wait on labs etc) and emergent surgical decompression

## Slide 61: Sheehan syndrome

- Sheehan syndrome, also known as postpartum pituitary necrosis, is a condition where the pituitary gland is damaged, often due to significant blood loss during or after childbirth. This damage leads to a decreased production of pituitary hormones, resulting in various symptoms.
- Causes:
- Postpartum hemorrhage/ hypovolemic shock:
- Severe blood loss during or after childbirth is the primary cause, leading to ischemia (reduced blood supply) and necrosis (tissue death) of the pituitary gland.
- Symptoms:
- Sheehan syndrome can manifest with a range of symptoms due to the deficiency of pituitary hormones, including:
- Inability to breastfeed (agalactorrhea): Prolactin, a hormone necessary for milk production, is often affected. -- First symptom
- Amenorrhea (absence of menstruation): Gonadotropin hormones, which regulate menstruation, are also deficient.
- Hypothyroidism: Thyroid-stimulating hormone deficiency can lead to hypothyroidism symptoms like fatigue, cold intolerance, and weight gain.
- Adrenal insufficiency: ACTH deficiency can lead to low cortisol levels, causing fatigue, weakness, and other symptoms.
- Hypogonadism: Gonadotropin deficiency can affect sex drive and reproductive function.

## Slide 62: Hypophysitis

- Hypophysitis-- pituitary gland inflammation, is a rare condition that can lead to hypopituitarism (decreased hormone production) and pituitary enlargement.
- Causes and Types:
- Primary Hypophysitis: -due to autoimmune conditions- ex: lymphocytic hypophysitis, IgG-4. Other types include granulomatous, xanthomatous, and plasmacytic hypophysitis.
- Secondary Hypophysitis: This is caused by other systemic diseases or conditions affecting the pituitary gland, such as infections or tumors.
- Immune Checkpoint Inhibitor-Related Hypophysitis
- Headaches are a common initial symptom.
- Visual disturbances due to compression of optic nerves or cranial nerves can occur.
- Hormonal deficiencies can lead to symptoms like adrenal insufficiency, hypothyroidism, hypogonadism, and growth hormone deficiency.
- Diabetes insipidus
