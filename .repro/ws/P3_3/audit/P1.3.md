    one BP id (the 812 "already covered" claims plus scope rejections that also point to a BP id) and 101 that cite none.
    Each cited BP line was read.
  - **Test.** A coverage claim holds only if the cited line(s), read literally, contain the concept, so that a topic
    author teaching exactly those lines would teach it. A named entity counts when the gap only restates it (for
    example "empty sella syndrome" or "Whipple triad"). A mechanism, lab pattern, presentation or drug effect that the
    line does not state does not count.
  - **When a claim failed:** the concept was ADDED as a new line (next free number in the topic that should teach it)
    when Step 1 teaches it at First Aid / Robbins / Costanzo level, or when the course lectures teach it (three lines
    cite a lecture: BP-rp12-15, BP-rp13-14 and BP-rp21-19). Otherwise the reason was rewritten truthfully: beyond Step 1
    emphasis, another organ system or discipline, or the BP ids that really cover it. Several GAP ids describing one
    concept share one new line (for example GAP-en2-05, GAP-en3-10 and GAP-en7-01 all become BP-en5-15).
  - **Verifier rows.** GAP-rp24-01 (breast density and alcohol) is now ADDED as BP-rp24-15. GAP-rp13-15 (preterm birth
    after LEEP) is now ADDED as BP-rp13-14. BP-rp13-06 does not cover it, and the course teaches it: the SIM-COLPO
    lecture notes (`scope/lectures/70351_23274306.md`, slide 26) say LEEP can still lead to cervical incompetence and

    were skimmed. Three were false and are fixed: GAP-rp10-09 and GAP-rp10-10 (the pelvic-infection risk at IUD

  - **Blueprint rules kept.** New lines use the header format, lowercase American-spelling key terms (2-4 per line,
    written as substrings a correct explanation must contain, `a|b` where spellings vary), and the next free number
    in each topic. No existing line was edited, deleted or marked `[x]`. BP-GAPS rows were rewritten in place and
    none was removed. Both files keep their original line endings.
