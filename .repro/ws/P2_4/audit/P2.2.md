## 6. P2.V F6 fix

The phase-2 verifier failed F6 (and with it N2) on two defects. Both are fixed in `engine/engine.js`; nothing else
changed.

**(1) The spacing ceiling could land in the past.** `examCeiling()` returned the exam minus 48 h whenever the exam was
still ahead. Inside the last 48 h that time is already gone. So a correct answer got a due date in the past and stayed
due for ever.

- Now: no date or a passed exam gives `Infinity` (no cap). The exam minus 48 h is used while that is still ahead.
  Inside the last 48 h the last pass lands halfway between now and the exam.
- So the ceiling is always after now and before the exam.
- All four due computations go through it: `promote` (rapid, recall grid), practice `due`, practice `verifyDue`, and the
  hesitant-rapid hold-back. Each is `Math.min(now + gap, examCeiling())` with gap > 0, so none can be in the past.

**(2) The date could not be typed.** Chrome fires `change` on every segment edit, and the handler called `render()`.
That rebuilt the input, and `restoreFocus()` put the caret back on the first segment every time.

- Now a complete valid date commits at once. `renderAround(exIn)` builds the view off screen and swaps every sibling
  at each level from the input up to `#app`. The input itself is never detached, so its value, segment and focus
  survive. The countdown, plan head, banner, plan rows and calendar are all rebuilt.
- A half-typed value (`""` or a year below 2000, such as the 0002 / 0020 / 0202 steps of typing a year) commits
  nothing. It snaps back to the stored date only on blur or Enter.
- `parseExamDate` now rejects years below 2000. Before, `new Date(2, ...)` quietly became 1902.

**(3) Exam-day wording, found while testing "exam today".** The exam is stored at 08:00. After 08:00 on the exam day,
the header said "Exam day" but the plan said "exam date passed" and showed the Reschedule banner.

- New `examDateGone()` means only a date before today has passed. The header, plan head and banner all ask it.
- On the exam day the plan head reads "the stub exam is today" and no banner shows.
- The probe's and the self-test's five-days-ago case still shows the banner.

## 7. P2.V F4 fix

Reopened 2026-09-27 because the third phase-2 verification failed F4. What the verifier saw: the plan head read
"4 stages left (~0.8 h) -> 2 more days at this pace" at paces 2, 3, 5 and 14 alike. Stepping `todaysPlan()` one day at a
time, pace 3 took 3 days and paces 5 and 14 took 4. On day 3 of a 5-day pace the head said 1 more day while days 3 and
4 were still left. The image row also read "1 images you have not identified".

**Cause.** `planHTML` worked out the days left as minutes left divided by the day's budget. That budget never drops
below 30 min, but `todaysPlan` holds new material to `perDay` and always gives the current stage a place. So the head
and the plan used two different rules.

**Fix (engine/engine.js, engine/selftest.js, engine/ENGINE-GLOBALS.md).**

- `stageAllot(left, paceLeft, dueRapid, linkedN)` is the one rule for how many stages a day takes. It holds the old
  arithmetic unchanged: `perDay`, the 30 to 300 min budget, the due and linked minutes, `stageBudget`, the last day
  takes everything, and stop rather than skip. `todaysPlan()` now schedules its stage rows with it. `paceLeftNow()` is
  the old `paceLeft` expression.
- `planDaysLeft()` steps `stageAllot` forward one day at a time from the current state. Each step takes one day off
  the pace, with today's due and linked counts held. It returns the days the plan takes, today included. It also
  returns the heaviest of those days in minutes and which day that is.
- The plan head now reads "N days to finish, today included · done on day D of P", or "finishes today, day D of P",
  or "every stage done". N is in `data-plandays`. D is `blockDay() + N - 1` and P is the chosen pace. So a stub pace of
  14 now says "4 days to finish ... done on day 4 of 14" instead of hiding the early finish. The stub has only 4 stages
  and a stage is never split.
- The 5 h warning used to fire on "days needed > pace". On a day under 5 h that printed "puts 4.4 h of work in today,
  past the 5 h/day ceiling". It now fires when today's rows total over 300 min, or when a coming day of the same
  projection does. The second case names that day: "puts about 13.3 h of work on day 2". That is pace 2 on a
  48-topic path, where the last day takes everything left.
- Singular and plural in the plan, calendar and Weak Spots text:
  - "1 image you have not identified".
  - The due row: "1 of these is from ...".
  - Calendar day title: "1 activity".
  - Weak Spots reason: "missed 1 of 1 question here".
  - The button: "1 targeted question".
  - Error-type method card: "1 of your 1 wrong attempt was saved without a time, so it can never be called a misread",
    "1 of the N wrong options ... is", and "1 of your 1 wrong attempt (100%) sits on one".
  - Every other count in those views was already guarded, or has a floor of 2 or more (pairs n >= 2, leeches >= 3,
    sure n >= 4, thread topics >= 2).
- The page self-test gained `paceHeader`, which is asserted in `R.ok`. It steps the plan for paces 1 to 14 twice:
  once with no review, and once with a standing load of 6 due items from another block plus 2 linked items. Every
  day it compares the head's `data-plandays` with the days the stepping still takes.
- Negative control: a scratch engine with the old head formula put back fails the self-test ("FAIL 1 errors"). It
  reports exactly the verifier's numbers: pace 3 said 2,2,1 and took 3; paces 5 and 14 said 2,2,1,1 and took 4.
- The pacing itself is unchanged. The probe still gives `paceDays` {2:2, 3:3, 5:4, 7:4, 10:4, 14:4} and a review
  share at 14 days of 0.43.

## 9. P2.V F8 follow-up: two false statements fixed

The orchestrator asked for these two before close. Both were in the section 8 report as seen but not changed.
Files changed: `engine/engine.js`, `engine/selftest.js`.

**(1) The linked-queue notice after a missed question.**

- **Was.** The notice read "Queued for review: N rapid items on <topic> added to your linked queue". N was
  `RAPID.filter(r=>r.c===q.c).length`, every rapid item on the topic. `wirePractice` queues only the keyword
  matches, at most 6, or the topic's first 4 when nothing matches. On real content, with about 20 items a topic,
  the notice overstated the queue several times over. It also said "added" when the items were already queued.
- **Now.** `wirePractice` records what this miss queued on the set: `ps.linkQ = {id, n: add.length, fresh}`, where
  fresh is how many were not in the queue before. A right answer and Next both clear it. The notice prints n and says
  one of three things:
  - "added to your linked queue" when all n are new;
  - "in your linked queue (k newly added, m already there)" when some are new;
  - "was / were already in your linked queue" when none are new.
- The High yield only clause from section 8 is kept.

**(2) `blockDay()` dropped by one after the first plan-row click of a fresh day.**

- **Was.** The rule was `used + (S.days[today] ? 0 : 1)`, where used counts only days with activity. A plan-row click
  writes today's record with acts 0, so the row can be ticked. That record cancelled the +1, but acts 0 kept today
  out of used. So "day 2 of this pace" turned into "day 1", `paceLeftNow()` grew by a day, and the plan re-spread
  the work.
- **Now.** Days before today count when they have activity (and are on or after the pace start), and today counts
  once, always: `min(paceDays, before + 1)`.
- The result is the same as before when today has activity or no record at all. Pacing and `paceHeader` are
  unchanged: the stub still gives `paceDays` {2:2, 3:3, 5:4, 7:4, 10:4, 14:4} and `paceHeader` bad `[]`.
