# YIELD — two separate yield axes per topic

Set by task P1.4 on 2026-09-26. Topics copy these values into `yld` (= `step1`) and `exam` (REPRO-PLAN.md §7.2); the gate
checks that each topic's values match this table.

- **step1** — how often USMLE Step 1 tests the topic: from `scope/STEP1-BLUEPRINT.md` (item count and the share marked hi)
  and NBME question patterns. The blueprint marks more than half of the items `hi` in 46 of the 48 topics, so the counts
  alone do not separate topics. `hi` is kept for topics that NBME forms ask about in several distinct ways. Physiology topics whose
  facts are tested mainly through the disorder topics built on them get `mid`.
- **exam** — how likely the topic is to be tested on the **BSE 638 final (Fri 10/9/2026)**. The rating uses session count and
  length in `scope/CANVAS-SCOPE.md`, BiCEP case load, the week each quiz signals in `scope/QUIZ-SIGNALS.md`, and lecture
  emphasis in `scope/lectures/`. The scope files do not say whether the final is cumulative. They say only that the
  9/11 midterm covered weeks 1–4. **Assumption** (see `audit/P1.4.md`): the final is weighted toward the weeks 5–8
  material that has not yet been examined, and it may revisit weeks 1–4. So topics taught mainly in weeks 5–8 get `hi`
  when the course spent a full session or more on them. Weeks 1–4 topics get `mid`, and `hi` only when week 5–8 sessions
  come back to them. For endocrine topics (not on the repro final), `exam` rates how much the BSE 612 endocrine week
  (7/27–7/31) taught the topic: `hi` for a quiz or TBL focus, `mid` for a lecture objective with slides, and `lo` for
  none. en10 is the one exception: the BSE 638 menopause lecture puts osteoporosis on the repro final. rp11 teaches it
  before the final, and en10 shares it.
