## Slide 25: Cervical Bx vs “Cold-Knife” Cone:

Notes:
Cervical biopsies do not cause problems with cervical incompetence and miscarriage. A cold knife cone can absolutely increase the risk of cervical incompetence and subsequent miscarriage

## Slide 26: Loop electrosurgical excision procedure: LEEP

Notes:
LEEP help to minimize risk of cervical incompetence while also providing treatments for cervical dysplasia or “pre-cancer”. However LEEPs can still lead to cervical incompetence and miscarriage in the future for some women and LEEPs are not without any risk to the patient. Primary prevention of HPV via vaccination is the best option
