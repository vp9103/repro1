## 5. Facts double-checked (second reading against standard Step 1 teaching, no second model)

- Ultrasound: hemorrhagic cyst reticular (lace-like) echoes with retracting clot and no flow; endometrioma homogeneous low-level (ground-glass) echoes; dermoid echogenic Rokitansky nodule with shadowing; torsion enlarged edematous ovary with peripheral follicles and whirlpool sign, Doppler flow can persist; postmenopausal stripe 4 mm or less (ACOG).
- O-RADS US risk bands 2 (under 1 percent), 3 (1 to under 10), 4 (10 to under 50), 5 (50 or more); color score 1 to 4; category 4 findings are described generally ("a few papillary projections") rather than by an exact count because the count is the least certain detail. IOTA Simple Rules (5 benign, 5 malignant; only benign = benign, only malignant = malignant, otherwise inconclusive); RMI = ultrasound score x menopausal score (1 or 3) x CA-125, above 200 refers (UK cutoff).
- Ovary: surface epithelium from coelomic epithelium; primordial germ cells reach the genital ridge in week 5 to 6; epithelial tumors about 90 percent of malignant ovarian tumors; HGSC from STIC, TP53, BRCA; type I KRAS/BRAF/PTEN; Krukenberg bilateral signet-ring from stomach; Meigs right pleural effusion; BRCA1 about 40 to 60 and BRCA2 about 10 to 20 percent lifetime ovarian risk against 1 to 2 percent (course slide 12); RRSO at 35 to 40 (BRCA1) and 40 to 45 (BRCA2); USPSTF 2018 recommends against ovarian cancer screening (as of 2025); OCs of 5 or more years cut risk about half; salpingectomy instead of tubal ligation (ACOG 2019). FOXL2 in adult granulosa cell tumors, DICER1 with Sertoli-Leydig tumors, STK11 with sex cord tumors with annular tubules.
- Gynecologic hub: unopposed estrogen and atypical hyperplasia, serous endometrial carcinoma TP53 in atrophy; HPV E6 degrades p53, E7 inactivates Rb; vulvar HPV 16 versus lichen sclerosus with TP53 (differentiated VIN); in utero DES, adenosis, clear cell adenocarcinoma; sarcoma botryoides in infants; wide local excision with sentinel node for vulvar cancer.
- Breast benign: age bands, fibrocystic change nonproliferative components, relative risks 1.5 to 2 (proliferative without atypia) and 4 to 5 (atypia), radial scar, fibroadenoma intra- versus pericanalicular, phyllodes behavior (hematogenous spread), mastitis organisms and drugs, implant complications and BIA-ALCL (CD30+, ALK-negative, textured), tuberous breast, Poland syndrome, gynecomastia ages and causes; ACR imaging by age.
- Breast cancer: TDLU origin; ADH versus DCIS 2 mm boundary; LCIS about 1 percent a year, pleomorphic LCIS excised; DCIS about 1 in 5 screen-detected, about 3 percent breast cancer death at 20 years; special-type frequencies; IHC and ISH rules for HER2; PAM50 four classes; pathologic complete response; Nottingham grade (tubules, nuclear pleomorphism, mitoses, each 1 to 3); BRCA breast cancer risk by 70 (about 55 to 65 and 45 to 50 percent), Ashkenazi founder variants about 1 in 40 versus about 1 in 400, PALB2 about 35 percent by 70, prophylactic mastectomy about 90 percent risk cut, RRSO 80 percent or more ovarian risk cut; USPSTF 2024 biennial mammography 40 to 74 (as of 2025); first-degree relative with colorectal cancer or advanced adenoma before 60 starts colonoscopy at 40 or 10 years before the relative's diagnosis; family history does not change cervical screening.
- Claims cut or kept general because of uncertainty: exact O-RADS 4 papillary-projection count; per-gene risk for TP53 and PTEN; KIT and 12p changes in dysgerminoma; HER2-low.
