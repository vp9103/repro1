## 4. Facts double-checked (no second reviewer; every number rechecked against the standard source)

- ACOG Practice Bulletin 222 (2020): thresholds 140/90 twice, 4 hours apart; proteinuria 300 mg/24 h or ratio 0.3; severe features (160/110, platelets under 100,000, creatinine over 1.1 mg/dL or doubled, transaminases twice normal, pulmonary edema, new headache or visual change); proteinuria of 5 g and fetal growth restriction are not severe features (kept as grid decoys); magnesium through labor and 24 hours postpartum; first-line acute agents IV labetalol, IV hydralazine, immediate-release oral nifedipine within 30 to 60 minutes; delivery at 34 weeks or later with severe features, 37 weeks without; aspirin 81 mg started at 12 to 16 weeks (ACOG window 12 to 28, ideally before 16; USPSTF after 12 weeks).
- Gestational diabetes (ACOG, ADA Standards of Care 2025): two-step 50 g (130 to 140 mg/dL threshold) then 100 g with Carpenter-Coustan 95/180/155/140, two abnormal values; one-step 75 g with 92/180/153, one abnormal value; early testing for high risk; retest 4 to 12 weeks postpartum with a 75 g test; preconception A1c under 6.5 percent.
- Rh (ACOG): anti-D at 28 weeks and within 72 hours; 300 mcg standard dose not stated in text; Kleihauer-Betke sizes extra dose; MCA Doppler for sensitized pregnancies.
- Labor (ACOG/SMFM 2014 and Zhang): active phase from 6 cm; first-stage arrest 4 hours adequate contractions or 6 hours oxytocin with ruptured membranes; second-stage 3 hours (nulliparous) or 2 hours (multiparous), one hour more with an epidural; Bishop score 0 to 13 (dilation, effacement, station 0-3; consistency, position 0-2) with 8 or more favorable; NST 15 beats for 15 seconds twice in 20 minutes; AFI 5 to 25 cm; PPH 1000 mL cumulative or hypovolemic signs within 24 hours (ACOG 2017), vaginal average about 500 mL; delayed cord clamping 30 to 60 seconds (ACOG 2020); tachysystole more than 5 contractions in 10 minutes; ECV from 36 to 37 weeks; 3a/3b/3c tear grading (RCOG); midline episiotomy extends into the sphincter more often than mediolateral.
- HIV and breastfeeding: stated as "as of 2025 shared decision-making is accepted when viral load is suppressed" (US DHHS perinatal guidelines, 2023 update).
- Laterality and associations: Erb C5-C6 waiter's tip; Klumpke C8-T1 with Horner; methylergonovine avoided in hypertension; carboprost avoided in asthma; indomethacin only before 32 weeks (ductus closure, oligohydramnios); labetalol avoided in asthma; ACE inhibitors avoided (fetal kidney); percreta passes through the myometrium and may reach the bladder.
- American spelling throughout (gate check clean).
- Claims cut because I was not sure: exact magnesium serum levels for toxicity, dose figures for uterotonics and anti-D, percentages of HELLP without hypertension, week ranges for scheduled cesarean in previa, accreta and vasa previa.
