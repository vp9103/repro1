## Page 16

Weight Gain/Unintentional Weight Loss
• There is a small number of medications that have been used to
stimulate appetite, but adverse effects limit there use, moreover,
there are no data on the long-term efficacy for improving quality of
life or reducing morbidity/mortality.
• Dronabinol, an orally active cannabinoid, is FDA approved for the stimulation
of appetite in patients with HIV
• Megesterol, a derivative of progesterone
• Cyproheptadine, an antihistamine

## Page 17

Key points/summary
• The regulation of appetite and satiety is complex and involves the
integration of many signals.
• The history of weight loss medications is checkered with product
rejections and withdrawals.
• The seven anti-obesity medications approved by the FDA are
phentermine, orlistat, phentermine/topiramate ER,
naltrexone/bupriopion SR, liraglutide, semaglutide and tirzepatide.
• Adverse effects of anti-obesity medications lead to discontinuation.
• GLP-1-induced satiety is via intestinal innervation; dipeptidyl
peptidase-4 (DPP-4) inactivates GLP-1
• GLP-1 receptor agonists can often cause rapid weight loss
