## 4. Facts double-checked (no second model reviews this work)

- Contraception: CDC US SPR 2024 quick start (7 days of backup unless within 5 days of menses, 2 days for the progestin-only pill) and the missed-pill rule (take the latest missed pill, 7 days of backup when two or more are missed); typical-use failure 13 (male condom), 17 (diaphragm), 7 (pill, patch, ring), 4 (DMPA), under 1 (implant, IUDs), perfect-use pill 0.3 per 100; patch less reliable above about 90 kg; ring and patch components (etonogestrel, norelgestromin); vasectomy semen analysis at about 3 months; levonorgestrel 72 hours and before the LH surge, ulipristal and copper IUD 5 days, BMI of 30 or more weakens levonorgestrel; mifepristone 200 mg then misoprostol 800 mcg buccal at 24 to 48 hours through 70 days of gestation (FDA label, as of 2025).
- Menopause: median age 51; STRAW anchor at the final menstrual period; early transition cycle length varying by 7 days or more, late transition gaps of 60 days or more; primary ovarian insufficiency FSH above about 25 twice at least 4 weeks apart, replacement to about 51; FSH above about 30 and estradiol below about 20 in the course deck; ACOG endometrial sampling from age 45 and in postmenopausal bleeding, 4 mm stripe; WHI facts (age 50 to 79, mean 63, combined arm stopped 2002, estrogen-alone arm without breast cancer excess); fezolinetant boxed warning for liver injury (December 2024); paroxetine strong CYP2D6 inhibitor with tamoxifen; November 2025 FDA announcement on boxed warnings is quoted from the course deck and labeled with its date.
- Gender-affirming care and ethics: Endocrine Society hematocrit threshold about 54 percent; spironolactone potassium; GnRH agonist flare then downregulation, reversible; prostate screening 55 to 69 (USPSTF shared decision), PSA lowered by estrogen and antiandrogens; Belmont Report 1979 and Tuskegee 1932 to 1972; Black maternal mortality about three times White; conscientious objection with a duty to refer; lecture figures (up to 26 percent refused care, 4 in 10 suicide attempts) kept as "up to a quarter" and "about 4 in 10".
- Left out because unsure: elinzanetant, the lecture's potency range for compounded hormones, exact state counts for banned transgender care (kept "more than 20 states, as of 2025").
