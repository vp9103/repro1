MINERALOCORTICOIDS - REVIEW
• Regulate sodium and water homeostasis
• Enhance Na+ and H2O reabsorption in the kidney,
colon and salivary glands
• Mediated by ENaC, glucocorticoid-regulated kinase
(sgk) and the Na+/K+ ATPase
• Regulates sodium and potassium transport in
the kidney
• MR has similar intrinsic affinity for
aldosterone, cortisol, and corticosterone
• Expressed in may tissues

## Page 14

Key points/summary
• Hypoadrenalism is caused by disruption of the hypothalamus, pituitary, or
adrenal glands
• Corticosteroids are given in physiologic doses for replacement therapy
• When given in supraphysiologic doses for prolonged periods, may cause decreased secretion
of endogenous corticosteroids by suppressing pituitary release of corticotropin (2º
adrenocortical insufficiency).
• Drug of choice is hydrocortisone
• Patients with primary adrenal insufficiency typically present with marked
impairment of aldosterone secretion
• Mineralocorticoid replacement is required for full recovery
• Mineralocorticoid therapy with fludrocortisone is required in 1º but not 2º adrenal
insufficiency
• Aldosterone production is intact in 2º, but not 1º adrenal insufficiency
• Patients with secondary adrenal insufficiency should receive replacement cortisol
• During times of stress, patients with known adrenal insufficiency should received
increased doses of glucocorticoid replacement
• Adrenal crisis requires immediate attention. Intravenous hydrocortisone is given,
along with fluids, immediately.
