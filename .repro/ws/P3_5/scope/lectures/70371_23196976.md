## Page 21

Key points/summary
• The most common cause of hyperthyroidism is Graves disease.
• Pharmacologic treatment of hyperthyroidism should be individualized.
• Hyperthyroidism can be treated with PTU or MMI
• They inhibit the synthesis of TH by preventing the incorporation of I into iodotyrosines and by
inhibiting the coupling of monoiodotyrosine and diiodotyrosine to form T4 and T3
• Unlike PTU, MMI does not inhibit the peripheral deiodination of thyroxine to triiodothyronine.
• Propylthiouracil has a boxed warning of severe liver injury and acute liver
failure, with some fatalities reported.
• The presentation of thyroid storm typically involves thyrotoxicosis as an
underlying condition, along with a precipitating agent, such as surgery or
the use of amiodarone
• Hyperpyrexia and cardiovascular and CNS dysfunction occurs
