## Slide 3: CASE 1(1)

- A 15 year old boy is seen by a primary care physician for short stature and delayed sexual development. His PMH is remarkable only for asthma in early childhood, which has been well controlled. He uses no medications.
- He is an average student in the 9th grade and smallest in his class. His older classmates harass him about his size. His parents are concerned because he is becoming withdrawn and a “loner”.
- Pregnancy and delivery were uncomplicated. H5 was born 8.5 lbs and 20 inches long. Head circumference was 35 cm. Immunizations are current.
- Adapted from: Case Based Pediatrics for Medical Students and Residents, Department of Pediatrics, University of Hawaii John A. Burns School of Medicine, Sherrel L. Hammar, MD. March 2002

## Slide 4: CASE 1 (2)

- FH: Mother 48 yo, in good health. Ht is 167 cm (50th %ile) and underwent menarche at age 13. Father 51 yo, in good health, Ht 184 cm (75th %ile). He was shaving regularly by age 15. One older male sibling in good health began puberty at age 11. No FH of smoking, alcoholism, mental illness, drug abuse, or learning disabilities.
- Exam: VS T 37.1, P 110, R 32, BP 100/60, HT 158 cm (3rd %tile) Wt. 42 kg (10th %tile). He is pale and anxious but cooperative. HEENT, Chest, Heart, and pulse exams normal. No abdominal tenderness or mass. Genitalia: normal circumcised phallus, Tanner pubic hair stage 1, genital stage 2. Testes firm, 2.5 cm in length and 4 ml in volume. Scrotum reddened and stippled. Otherwise physical exam is normal.
- Lab: CBC, Hb 13, WBC 9.5, normal differential, platelets 350,000, UA normal.
- Adapted from: Case Based Pediatrics for Medical Students and Residents, Department of Pediatrics, University of Hawaii John A. Burns School of Medicine, Sherrel L. Hammar, MD. March 2002

## Slide 7: CASE 2 (1)

- A 6-year girl is brought for evaluation of breast enlargement. She weighs 25 kg (90th percentile) and is 128 cm (>95th percentile). She has enlarged breasts, sexual maturity rating 2-3, with pigmentation of the areola and hyperpigmented macules.
- A pelvic ultrasound reveals a left ovarian cyst 3.7 x 3.4 cm. The uterus is enlarged with a length of 4.6 cm (normal < 3 cm) with a prominent endometrium.
- Bone age is consistent with age 8 years. Estradiol is elevated at 113 pg/ml (normal <36 pg/ml), and LH 1.7 mIU/ml (normal <1 mIU/ml). DHEAS and FSH are normal. The patient complains of pain in her lower limb. Area radiographs are shown.
- Adapted from: Moein Mobini; Rahim Vakili; Saba Vakili, International J Pediatr Volume 2, Issue 2.2, April 2014, pp 153-156

## Slide 10: CASE 3 (1)

- A 12-year-old boy is concerned about a lack of pubertal development during a recent annual physical exam. Although he is currently healthy, his medical history is notable for lack of midfacial fusion, including cleft lip and palate involving the nasal structure at birth, for which he underwent multiple surgeries. He was found to be anosmic postoperatively in early childhood.
- His growth chart reveals he has been consistently along the 10th percentile for height. As he approached the usual age of the pubertal growth spurt (12 years), he continue to grow 5 cm annually.
- His current height and weight are at the 25th and 10th percentiles for age, respectively. Vital signs and physical exam are normal, demonstrating good results from his facial surgeries. He has small, firm testes, 1 cc in volume bilaterally, and < 1 cm in length. His penis is prepubertal, and he has no sexual hair.
- Adapted from: Medscape Pediatrics, authors PA Lee and CP Houk
