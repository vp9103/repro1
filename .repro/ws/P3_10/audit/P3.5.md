## 4. Facts double-checked (no second model: first-line review)

- Vagina and vulva: glycogen to lactic acid by lactobacilli, pH 3.8 to 4.5; the vaginal wall has no glands (lubrication is a transudate from the subepithelial venous plexus plus cervical and Bartholin mucus); Bartholin ducts open at about 4 and 8 o'clock (some sources say 5 and 7; the lecture slide says 5 and 7, so "about" is kept); Bartholin duct has transitional epithelium (lecture slide); Skene glands homologous to the prostate; Gartner duct is a mesonephric remnant; Word catheter or marsupialization; biopsy of a persistent Bartholin mass after 40; Amsel 3 of 4; CDC 2021: metronidazole 500 mg twice daily for 7 days for bacterial vaginosis and for trichomoniasis in women, retest within 3 months, partners treated for trichomoniasis, symptomatic pregnant women treated for both, no screening of asymptomatic pregnant women for vaginosis, no alcohol restriction with metronidazole; fluconazole 150 mg once, topical azoles in pregnancy, partners not treated, 3 or more episodes a year are recurrent, non-albicans species (C. glabrata) get vaginal boric acid; cervicitis: doxycycline with ceftriaxone when gonorrhea is likely, retest at about 3 months; lichen sclerosus (thin epidermis, loss of rete ridges, dermal homogenization, bandlike lymphocytes, small SCC risk), lichen simplex chronicus (acanthosis, hyperkeratosis, no atypia), lichen planus (erosive, vaginal and oral); HPV-associated HSIL (usual VIN: p16 block positive, wild-type p53) versus HPV-independent dVIN (basal atypia, aberrant p53, p16 negative or patchy, lichen sclerosus); vulvar SCC to superficial inguinal nodes first; sentinel node or inguinofemoral lymphadenectomy when invasion exceeds 1 mm (FIGO IA is 1 mm or less); EMPD CK7 and CEA positive, S100 and HMB-45 negative, usually no underlying carcinoma; DES, adenosis, clear cell adenocarcinoma; sarcoma botryoides under 5 years, desmin, myogenin, MyoD1, cambium layer.
- Cervix: ectocervix nonkeratinized squamous, endocervix columnar with plicae palmatae; estrogen everts the columnar lining; ectropion with oral contraceptives, pregnancy, adolescence; stenosis then hematometra; cervical insufficiency after cone or LEEP; SCC about three-quarters, adenocarcinoma linked to HPV 18; hydronephrosis from parametrial extension; 9-valent vaccine types 6, 11, 16, 18, 31, 33, 45, 52, 58, age 11 or 12, 2 doses before 15, catch-up to 26, shared decision 27 to 45 (as of 2025).
- Screening (as of 2025): USPSTF 2018 cytology every 3 years from 21 to 29; 30 to 65 primary HPV every 5 years, cotest every 5 years or cytology every 3 years; ACS 2020 primary HPV every 5 years from 25 to 65 (cotest or cytology acceptable where HPV testing is unavailable), nothing before 25; stop after 65 with adequate negative screening (ACS: no CIN 2 or worse in 25 years); stop after total hysterectomy for benign disease with no CIN 2 or worse; ASCCP 2019: management by risk of CIN 3 or worse, colposcopy at about 4 percent immediate risk, ASC-US with reflex HPV (positive: colposcopy; negative: 3 years), LSIL with a recent negative HPV test: repeat in 1 year, AGC: colposcopy with ECC plus endometrial biopsy at 35 or older or with risk factors; unsatisfactory cytology repeated in 2 to 4 months; ablation only with a fully visible lesion, a negative canal and no suspicion of invasion; cold-knife cone for suspected glandular disease; HIV: screening from within a year of sexual debut, annual cytology until three normal results then every 3 years, lifelong, anal cytology then high-resolution anoscopy; USPSTF hepatitis C once at 18 to 79 and CDC in each pregnancy, HIV 15 to 65, mammography every 2 years at 40 to 74, colorectal 45 to 75.
- Uterus: hyperplasia without atypia 1 to 3 percent cancer risk, atypia (EIN) high risk with PTEN loss; type I about 80 percent, 55 to 65 years, PTEN and mismatch repair, indolent; type II 65 to 70, atrophic endometrium, TP53, psammoma bodies, aggressive; FIGO IA under half, IB half or more of the myometrium; Lynch: MLH1 and MSH2, endometrium the commonest extracolonic site; Cowden: PTEN, breast, thyroid, endometrium; carcinosarcoma biphasic, homologous or heterologous elements, usually TP53 mutated, tamoxifen risk; leiomyoma 2 to 3 times more common in Black women, never becomes sarcoma; leiomyosarcoma three-feature diagnosis, hematogenous spread to the lung; adenomyosis in multiparous women in their 40s; endometriosis, GnRH agonist or antagonist, danazol effects and hereditary angioedema; postmenopausal bleeding: 4 mm ultrasound threshold, about 1 in 10 with cancer, atrophy commonest.
