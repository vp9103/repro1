- **Term conventions.** Terms are matched as lowercase substrings of the rendered text, so:
  - Stems cover inflections: `atypi`, `koilocyt`, `thromboembol`, `contracepti`, `hypothalam`, `viriliz`.
  - Names with a diacritic or an apostrophe are given by their plain part: `paramesonephric` or `wolffian` for
    (Müllerian), `health initiative` for the Women's Health Initiative, `hippel` for von Hippel–Lindau, and
    `reductase deficiency` for 5α-/5-alpha-reductase deficiency.
  - Abbreviations of 4 letters or fewer are written out: `chorionic gonadotropin` for hCG, `pertussis` for Tdap,
    `gliflozin` for SGLT2 inhibitors, `ca-125`, `loop electrosurgical` for LEEP.
  - **No row uses `a|b`.** The row regex in `repro_common.topic_map_rows` (and `topic_map`) reads the terms cell as
    `([^|]*?)`, so a `|` inside the cell counts as a column break and the whole row fails to parse. `topicmap_ok` would
    then report "no TOPIC-MAP row". I tested this, and it is also noted in the file header. Every term is therefore a
    single spelling, and topic authors must use that exact form at least once.

### 1. Method

- Read each objective in `scope/CANVAS-SCOPE.md`, split it into its named parts (define, list, explain, compare, apply), and read the
  session's deck in `scope/lectures/` wherever the objective's depth or the course's emphasis was unclear (the pelvis, perineum, male
  histology and pathology, prostate and penis, GTD, menopause, transgender, weight management, adnexal and TBL STI decks were re-read).
- Chose 2-4 terms per row so that each named part has an anchor a topic can only contain by teaching that part: drug names, findings,
  mechanisms, named complications and structures, not generic words, not words that also match unrelated text in the same topic (checked
  against the current fast text: a term with dozens of hits was replaced), and not merely a copy of the topic's STEP1-BLUEPRINT keys. A
  blueprint key is kept in a row only when the part it anchors is named in the objective and no better word exists; every changed row has at
  least one term that is not a key of its first topic.
- Where a part is already forced by a blueprint key or by a sibling row of the same objective (lecture and lab, TBL and BiCEP twins), the
  row does not repeat it; sibling rows were chosen to complement each other (for example ANAT-PELVIS-LEC.5 and LAB.9, PHYS-MENSTRUAL.1-5).
- Where 2-4 terms could not anchor every part, the parts the course and Step 1 test most were anchored and the rest is listed in section 5
  as `UNANCHORED`.
- Facts checked twice (drug names, enzymes, eponyms, cutoffs, laterality). Time-bound items: RSV vaccine in pregnancy (PRENATAL-CARE.8)
  and the liver warning on fezolinetant (MENOPAUSE.7) are as of 2025. Terms are plain lowercase ASCII; no term has a `|`; each is at least
  5 characters or contains a digit (`shbg` was replaced by `binding globulin`, `pten` and `afp` are not used). A hyphen or space inside a term is
  part of the spelling (`tubal factor`, `e-cadherin`, `high-riding`); the topic must use that spelling.
