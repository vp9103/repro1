## 4. Facts double-checked (no second model: first-line review)

- Dating and weeks: 40 weeks or 280 days from the LMP, 38 weeks or 266 days from fertilization; hCG detectable about 1 week after fertilization; primary villi week 2, tertiary villi by the end of week 3, fetal heart beats week 4; anterior neuropore closes about day 25 and posterior about day 27 (week 4).
- Embryology: paraxial mesoderm gives somites (axial skeleton, trunk muscle, dermis); notochord leaves the nucleus pulposus; tracheoesophageal fistula with esophageal atresia, distal fistula the usual form, polyhydramnios; urachus is the median umbilical ligament, umbilical vein the ligamentum teres, arteries the medial umbilical ligaments; Meckel diverticulum is a vitelline duct remnant.
- Twins: lambda (twin peak) sign is dichorionic, T sign is monochorionic diamniotic; zygosity timings 0-4, 4-8, 8-12, 13 or more days; dizygotic rate rises with maternal age and assisted reproduction, monozygotic about 4 per 1000.
- Placental hormones and abnormal implantation: hCG rescues the corpus luteum, luteal-placental shift at 8 to 10 weeks; hPL lipolysis spares glucose for the fetus; previa is painless, abruption is painful; accreta, increta, percreta defined by depth.
- Sex development: SRY switches on SOX9 (Sertoli cells, AMH); INSL3 from Leydig cells drives the transabdominal phase and androgen the inguinoscrotal phase; deep inguinal ring lies in the transversalis fascia lateral to the inferior epigastric vessels, superficial ring in the external oblique aponeurosis; cremasteric reflex afferent femoral branch, efferent genital branch of the genitofemoral nerve; appendix testis is a Müllerian remnant and its torsion gives the blue-dot sign; urorectal septum divides the cloaca; ectopic ureter opens above the sphincter in boys and below it in girls; septate uterus is the commonest anomaly and the one with recurrent pregnancy loss, bicornuate, didelphys and unicornuate carry preterm birth and breech; diethylstilbestrol use stopped in the early 1970s.
- Disorders: XYY from paternal meiosis II nondisjunction with normal fertility; 46,XX testicular DSD is SRY translocation with azoospermia; ovotesticular DSD most often 46,XX; Klinefelter azoospermia from hyalinized tubules, sperm retrievable in some; Swyer gonadectomy at diagnosis (gonadoblastoma), CAIS gonadectomy after puberty.
- Corrections made to the imported drafts: Swyer syndrome is not "most often" an SRY defect (now "classically ... many cases have no identified cause"); the CAH paragraph said "labioscrotal folds" (now swellings); the MRI paragraph made the whole myometrium dark on T2 (now dark junctional zone and intermediate-signal outer myometrium); the hPL sentence tied gestational diabetes to free fatty acids alone (now the insulin resistance).
- Cut as uncertain: the claim that AMH-driven Müllerian regression drives the transabdominal phase (the course slide says so; current texts credit INSL3, so the lesson teaches INSL3 and leaves the tethering by retained Müllerian structures to the existing sexp); surveillance intervals for monochorionic twins; the share of Swyer syndrome caused by SRY.
