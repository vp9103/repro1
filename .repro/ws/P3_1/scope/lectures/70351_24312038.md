- Her β-hCG is 2,400 mIU/mL. You get an ultrasound and it shows a gestational sac but no fetal pole.

What additional information would be most useful?
What is the most appropriate next step?

## Slide 5

- CASE 2
- Sources, date
- A 30 year old G3P0020 comes to see you for an appointment. LMP was 6 weeks ago so she took a pregnancy test and it was positive. She has also been having some left abdominal pain but no vaginal bleeding. Her β-hCG level is 1,500 mIU/mL. She has a transvaginal ultrasound that demonstrates no intrauterine pregnancy. You get a repeat β-hCG 48 hours later and it is now 1,850 mIU/mL.

## Slide 6

- QUESTION
- Is the pt pregnant?
- How pregnant?
- What additional information do you need?
- What is the differential diagnosis?
- What is the next best step?

Notes:
Q1: Is the pt pregnant?
Q2: How pregnant? Based on LMP? (8wks) Based on BHCG? (5-6 wks) so Is it normal? (probably not)
Q3: What additional information would be helpful? [Are her periods normal and how long is her menstrual cycle? (28-30 days) Has she had any sti infections in the past? Chlamydia in college) Has she ever been pregnant before? (Yes – 1 SAB, 1 ectopic s/p methotrexate and her current pregnancy) Is she stable? What are her vitals? (Pain is mild, she is not hypotensive, abdominal exam does not demonstrate guarding, normal pulse)].
Q4: What is on the DDx?
Q5: Next best step? Serial b-hcg vs f/u ultrasound? At what point do you move from observation to tx?
Teaching Tip: the object of this question is NOT to recognize an ectopic pregnancy but rather to manage diagnostic uncertainty while avoiding harm from both missed ectopic pregnancy and premature treatment of a potentially viable intrauterine pregnancy.

## Slide 7

- CASE 3
- Sources, date
- 34-year-old G4P1021 presents to clinic with vaginal bleeding and clots with cramping for the past 24 hrs. LMP was 10 wks ago. She was seen 2 weeks ago and at that visit had an ultrasound that confirmed an intrauterine pregnancy with a CRL consistent with 8 weeks and a fetal heart rate of 160.
- Today is a febrile, has a BP of 118/72 and pulse of 84.
- β-hCG:
- 2 weeks ago: 38,000 mIU/mL
- Today: 24,500 mIU/mL
- Ultrasound today: Intrauterine gestational sac present, embryo measures 8 weeks 2 days, no cardiac activity identified.

- What other information do you need?

- What do you tell the patient and her partner when they say “But we saw a heartbeat 2 wks ago.”

Notes:
Is the pt pregnant
How pregnant?
What pieces of information are most important in determining whether this pregnancy is viable? Another bhcg? Ultrasound?
What is the most likely diagnosis?
Discussion Points
Students frequently struggle with:
Distinguishing bleeding from pregnancy loss
Understanding that fetal cardiac activity may later cease
Communicating certainty and uncertainty compassionately

## Slide 9

- CASE 4
- A 21-year-old G1P0 presents to the emergency department for severe nausea and vaginal spotting. LMP was 11 weeks ago. She had a positive pregnancy test 7 wks ago.
- Over the past month she has had progressively worsening nausea and vomiting so much so she is having problems holding down even water, much less food. She has also had some intermittent vaginal bleeding but is not having any cramping.
- Today she is afebrile, BP 148/92 and a pulse of 110. You do a bimanual exam and feel like her uterus is larger than you would expect for what you would expect based on her LMP.
- You get a β-hCG and it is 250,000 IU and an ultrasound demonstrates “heterogeneous intrauterine material with multiple cystic spaces.” No fetus is identified within the uterine cavity.

## Slide 10

- QUESTIONS
- Is the pt pregnant?
- How pregnant?
- What aspect of this pt’s presentation to your clinic is the most concerning?
- What’s the diagnosis?
- Why are the hCG levels so high?
- What part of embryology has gone awry?
- You must ALWAYS follow the hCG to zero in gestational trophoblastic disease

Notes:
Is she pregnant? Yes
How pregnant?
What aspect of this is the most concerning? BP? Spotting? N/V?
What’s the diagnosis? Gestational trophoblastic disease
Why are the hCG levels so high? Excess trophoblastic proliferation
What part of embryology has gone awry?
Why do you have to follow the hcg to 0 even after she’s had a d&c?
