## Slide 39: MENSTRUAL SUPPRESSION

- GOAL: Decrease Frequency + Volume of Menses
- Complete Amenorrhea may be difficult
- Hormone Therapy doesn’t affect Future Fertility
- Hormone Therapy doesn’t increase Cancer risk

## Slide 40: MEDICAL OPTIONS: MENSTRUAL SUPPRESSION

- Combined OCPS: Daily up to 88% Amenorrhea
- Transdermal: Weekly Adhesive problems
- Vaginal Ring: Monthly High Amenorrhea rate
- Oral Progestins: Daily
- Depo Provera: 11-13 weeks Weight gain Affects Bones
- Nexplanon: 3 – 5 yrs Only 22% Amenorrhea rate
- Progestin IUD: 5-8 years Cost/Insertion Pain 60%
- GNRH agonist: Daily to 3 mo High Amenorrhea rate

## Slide 41: Testosterone Effects on the Menstrual Cycle

- 1. Cycles will stop within a few months of starting Testosterone
- 2. For Continued Bleeding – Use Progesterone
- 3. Testosterone causes Vaginal Atrophy
- Increased Risk: Bacterial vaginosis, Cystitis, Cervicitis, Dyspareunia
- Use Topicals – Lubricants, Vaginal moisturizers, Topical Estrogen

## Slide 51: Medical Transition –Feminizing Therapy

- Transdermal Estrogen is the safest /Best dosing
- Oral Estrogen – use 17-B Estradiol
