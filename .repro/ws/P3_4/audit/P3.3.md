## 4. Facts double-checked (no second model: first-line review)

- Puberty: the first hormonal change is a sleep-related rise in pulsatile LH, FSH in parallel (Bordini and Rosenfield, in the course readings); kisspeptin neurons sit in the arcuate nucleus; LH acts on theca and Leydig cells, FSH on granulosa and Sertoli cells; positive feedback matures late in girls only; peak height velocity about 8 cm a year in girls near breast stage 3 and about 9.5 cm a year in boys at genital stage 3 to 4; spermarche near genital stage 3 to 4; premature thelarche usually under 2 years; pubertal gynecomastia in about half of boys, fading in a year or two; central precocious puberty confirmed by a basal LH above roughly 0.3 mIU/mL (ultrasensitive assay) or a peak above roughly 5 mIU/mL after GnRH stimulation (Long, Pediatr Rev 2015, in the course readings; worded "roughly" because assay dependent); familial testotoxicosis is an activating LH-receptor mutation; precocious: before 8 in girls and 9 in boys; delayed: no thelarche by 13 or testes under 4 mL by 14.
- Ovary and cycle: 1 to 2 million follicles at birth, about 300,000 to 400,000 at puberty, about 400 ovulated; growth to the preantral stage does not need gonadotropins and the early antral stage depends on FSH; slow GnRH pulses favor FSH and fast pulses favor LH; desmolase (CYP11A1) makes pregnenolone, the rate-limiting step; LH peak near day 14 and ovulation about 10 to 12 hours later (34 to 36 hours after the surge begins); progesterone peak about day 21 with a second, smaller estradiol rise from the corpus luteum; implantation about 6 to 7 days after ovulation; luteal progesterone deciliates the tubal epithelium while estrogen raises ciliated cells (course slide 11); the perimetrium is the uterine serosa.
- Amenorrhea and bleeding: ACOG criteria for primary amenorrhea (15 with normal breasts, 13 without, the second also the definition of delayed puberty); POI is FSH above 25 mIU/mL on two samples at least 4 weeks apart before age 40 (ESHRE and ASRM; course slide 13); hormone therapy to about the age of natural menopause; heavy menstrual bleeding above 80 mL or longer than 7 days; oligomenorrhea above 35 days and polymenorrhea below 21 days as the course slides give them; endometrial biopsy first from age 45 or earlier with unopposed estrogen (ACOG); late-night salivary cortisol screens for Cushing syndrome; DHEA-S arises almost entirely from the adrenal cortex.
- Infertility: 12 months, or 6 months at 35 or older (ACOG, ASRM); HSG spill proves patency, hydrosalpinx fills without spill; filling defects are polyp, submucosal fibroid, adhesions or clot; intracytoplasmic sperm injection with surgical sperm retrieval for severe male factor; letrozole first-line for ovulation induction in PCOS.
- Cut as uncertain: LH and FSH numeric cutoffs by sex and assay beyond the two quoted; exact ages of spermarche by race; percentages of boys with a CNS lesion; the share of idiopathic POI; day-21 progesterone numeric threshold in the lesson text (the figure keeps "above 3 ng/mL").
- Changes to imported text: the draft sexp positions were 3, 1, 3 (now 2, 0, 1); the virilization call-out said DHEA-S points to "the adrenal gland" and testosterone to "the ovary" (now "an adrenal tumor" and "an ovarian tumor", the finding the objective names); the draft late-puberty and central-puberty paragraphs gave "LH after GnRH-agonist stimulation" and "brain MRI" (now the named GnRH stimulation test and magnetic resonance imaging, with the LH cutoffs); the draft trap call-out is rewritten around growth velocity.
