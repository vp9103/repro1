1 [c] Call-Exner bodies: These tiny, follicle-like spaces are ringed by tumor cells and contain eosinophilic (pink) secretions. They mimic normal developing follicles and are the classic diagnostic hallmark of granulosa cell tumors.
2 [e] Diffuse sheets of uniform cells: The tumor is composed of a monotonous population of small, round-to-oval cells with scant cytoplasm. The nuclei resemble normal granulosa cells and show minimal atypia.
3 [e] Fibrovascular stroma: This prominent band of pink collagenous tissue containing blood vessels runs diagonally, dividing the sheets of granulosa cells into lobules or nests.
