1 [e] Call-Exner body: this tiny follicle-like space contains eosinophilic fluid surrounded haphazardly by tumor cells. It recapitulates an ovarian follicle and serves as the classic hallmark of a granulosa cell tumor.
2 [e] Diffuse sheets of granulosa cells: monotonous, crowded cells with scant cytoplasm and round-to-oval nuclei dominate the parenchyma. Some nuclei display characteristic longitudinal indentations ('coffee-bean' nuclei).
3 [arrow] Fibrovascular stroma: a pink collagenous septal band bearing thin-walled vascular channels traverses diagonally across the field, dividing the hypercellular sheets of granulosa cells into lobules.
