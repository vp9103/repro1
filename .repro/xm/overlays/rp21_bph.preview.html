1 [e] Large hyperplastic prostate glands with characteristic papillary infoldings into the lumen. These glands are lined by a pseudostratified columnar epithelium with a pale secretory layer, representing epithelial overgrowth in benign prostatic hyperplasia (BPH).
2 [e] A central hyperplastic nodule composed of crowded, smaller, closely packed glandular acini. Hyperplasia in BPH is characteristically nodular and originates predominantly within the periurethral transition zone.
3 [arrow] Broad bands of pink fibromuscular stroma separating the hyperplastic glandular elements. BPH involves proliferation of both glandular epithelium and stromal smooth muscle/fibroblasts; alpha-1 adrenergic antagonists target this smooth muscle to reduce urethral resistance.
