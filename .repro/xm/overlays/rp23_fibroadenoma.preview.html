1 [e] Open round-to-oval ducts lined by orderly, regular epithelium and surrounded by concentric stroma. This preserved, patent architecture demonstrates the classic pericanalicular pattern of fibroadenoma.
2 [arrow] Ductal lumens compressed into elongated, branching purple slits by overgrowing stroma. This cleft-like architecture represents the intracanalicular pattern; both patterns often coexist in the same lesion.
3 [c] Abundant, pale pink stroma composed of bland, evenly distributed spindle fibroblasts without hypercellularity, nuclear atypia, or leaf-like fronds, effectively ruling out a phyllodes tumor.
4 [e] Green surgical margin ink, mature adipocytes, and a condensed fibrous border demarcate the sharp, non-infiltrative pushing edge responsible for the classic mobile, well-circumscribed lump on exam.
