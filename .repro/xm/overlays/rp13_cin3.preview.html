1 [r] Full-thickness atypia: Dark, crowded, and irregular nuclei fill the epithelium from the basal layer almost all the way to the surface. This involvement of the entire thickness of the epithelium is the hallmark of CIN 3 (HSIL) and distinguishes it from lower-grade lesions.
2 [e] Loss of maturation: Nuclei near the surface remain large, dark, and crowded. They fail to flatten or acquire abundant, glycogen-rich cytoplasm, which normally characterizes mature superficial squamous cells.
3 [arrow] Intact basement membrane: The epithelium rests on a clean, well-defined boundary with no nests of atypical cells invading the underlying stroma. This confirms it is carcinoma in situ (pre-invasive).
