1 [e] Single-file rows: Small, round tumor cells line up in single-file rows between collagen bundles. This characteristic 'indian filing' is due to the loss of E-cadherin, a cell-cell adhesion molecule, preventing them from forming cohesive nests or glands.
2 [e] Diffuse infiltration of fat: Individual tumor cells and thin cords infiltrate directly between mature adipocytes in the adipose tissue. Because they do not form a cohesive mass or induce a strong desmoplastic response, these tumors are often clinically and mammographically occult.
3 [arrow] Small uniform cells: The tumor cells are small, monotonous, and uniform, with round, dark, bland nuclei and very scant cytoplasm. They lack the marked nuclear pleomorphism, prominent nucleoli, and mitotic activity typically seen in invasive ductal carcinoma.
