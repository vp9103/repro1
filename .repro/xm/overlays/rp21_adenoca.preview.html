1 [r] Crowded small glands: closely packed small-to-medium glands with open lumens fill the needle core, demonstrating back-to-back crowding with minimal intervening stroma.
2 [e] Infiltrative pattern: malignant glands dissect haphazardly through pink fibromuscular stroma, wrapping around stromal fiber bundles rather than organizing into normal rounded lobules.
3 [arrow] Single cell layer: each malignant gland is lined by a single layer of cuboidal to columnar cells with dark nuclei. Cancer glands lack the outer basal cell layer present in benign glands, rendering p63 staining negative and AMACR positive.
4 [bracket] Needle core: the narrow curved strip of tissue with blank white background above and below is a needle biopsy core, which is the standard procedure for diagnosing prostate cancer after detecting a raised PSA level.
