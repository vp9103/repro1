Method
- Read in full, field by field (s, l, o, a, e, w, et, bl, pt.hl, pt.say for questions; q, o, a, x, w, pt for rapid items; key, items and whys for drills): fast/content/questions/<tid>.json (81 items), fast/content/rapid/<tid>.json (104 items) and fast/content/drills/d_<tid>_*.json (7 drills, 95 rows), 192 objects in all.
- Each item was judged against its current lesson fast/content/topics/<tid>.json (read in full, R1 and R3 repairs included) and every figure an item points to (caption, teach and SVG labels), and against First Aid, Pathoma, Robbins and Costanzo, plus dated guidance where an item depends on it: ACOG 2024 clinical practice update on Rh D immune globulin before 12 weeks (as taught in rp26 body[8]), ACOG/SRU early pregnancy loss criteria, ACOG Practice Bulletin 222 (postpartum blood pressure), USPSTF 2018 cervical screening, Endocrine Society 2017 and WPATH on testosterone effects.
- Rewording damage was checked item by item against the version before the practice pass (git 158eac3^ for rp24, rp26, rp27, rp30; 6e52424^ for rp25, rp28, rp29) and against fast/ws/P02/audit/P02-notes.md and fast/ws/P03/audit/P03-notes.md.
- "On the page now": QS, RAPID, RAPID_POINT, RAPID_MEDIA, DRILLS and DRILL_WHY were parsed out of fast/repro-endo-path.html (built 00:21) and compared with the content files. All 192 objects are identical (page w notes are keyed by option index and were compared that way; all 59 rapid pointers in RAPID_POINT match). Every pt.hl label exists in its figure. The page was served with python3 serve.py --root /home/user/repro1/fast --port 8818 (stopped afterwards) and driven with Node Playwright through the app's own state and render path: at a true 1280 px (light) practice items rp24q04, rp25q11, rp27q04, rp30q07, rp30q09 (confidence button, then a wrong option) and rapid items rp26r07, rp27r08, rp27r12, rp28r07, rp30r02; at a true 400 px (window.innerWidth 400, dark) rp24q05, rp27q04, rp29q01, rp24r14, rp26r07, rp27r12, rp30r06. The highlighted figure labels, say-lines and opened why-notes were read on the page. No page errors and no horizontal overflow.
- Kinds: ERROR (a wrong fact or causal claim), KEY, NON-STEP1, NOT-TAUGHT (not taught in this topic's lesson), WORDING, GIVEAWAY, POINTER (a pt.hl that marks the wrong or extra labels on the page).
- Conventions: fields are the JSON keys. Renaming an option means renaming its w key and the first cell of its et row too. Every fix quotes the text it replaces, keeps the item id and the keyed answer, and was word-counted against the §7.11 bands; every replacement pt.hl label was checked to match exactly one label of its figure with the engine's matching rule.
