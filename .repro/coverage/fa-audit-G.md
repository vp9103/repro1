# First Aid checklist audit G: rp1-rp15 against Step 1 sources

Auditor: fresh Claude (Opus), 2026-10-06. Coverage audit, not a phase verdict; no token. Nothing in content, engine, scope, audit, ledger, gate, plan or .claude/ was edited; this file is the only output.

Method
- Direction: from what Step 1 sources teach toward the lessons. For each topic I listed the First Aid entries that belong to its subject (the reproductive chapter's anatomy, embryology, physiology, pathology and pharmacology entries, plus the entries filed elsewhere that a question on the subject can test: renal (urinary incontinence), musculoskeletal (superior gluteal, obturator and pudendal nerves), GI anatomy (inguinal hernias, hepatic adenoma), heme/onc (paraneoplastic syndromes, fetal erythropoiesis), microbiology (vaginitis organisms), pharmacology (CYP450 inducers, SSRIs, leuprolide, danazol, SERMs) and psychiatry (PMDD)), added Pathoma ch. 14 (female genital tract) and Costanzo (reproductive physiology) where they teach more, and used the topic's STEP1-BLUEPRINT section as the floor. Sources are cited by book, chapter and entry (FA = First Aid for the USMLE Step 1, 2023-2025 editions, whose entries used here are unchanged across them); page numbers are omitted because the book is not available in this environment and could not be verified.
- Files read for every topic: fast/content/topics/<tid>.json (every body row, pretest, grid, self-explanation, why box), every placed figure (figs/<key>.json caption and teach; every text node and the aria-label of figs/<key>.svg), every placed image (images/<key>.json dx, look, findings, overlay labels), guides/<tid>.json and glossary/<tid>.json. md5 of every rp1-rp15 topic, glossary and figure file was recorded at the start and again before writing: unchanged (the 01:01 merge 99ae96e touched rp24 and rp27 only). The built fast/repro-endo-path.html was grep-checked and carries the run-3 fixes (for example "pleomorphic gram-variable rod"); the page was not served, because every finding is a content fact.
- Every checklist fact was searched in its own lesson and then across the whole course (all 30 rp lessons and the written en1, en2, en5, en6, en7 and en9 lessons, with their figures and glossaries). DUPLICATE: a fact that another rp topic already teaches is not requested; each topic lists those under "Not requested here". Facts taught only in an en lesson are still requested when the First Aid entry belongs to this topic (the rule names rp1-rp30), and that is noted in the row.
- Already fixed and not re-reported: every row of step1-audit-A.md and -B.md and the P3.V run-2 and run-3 findings; all confirmed present for rp1-rp15 (for example rp1 b9 and b22, rp12 b9 Gardnerella, b12 Trichomonas, b18 cervicitis "most often", rp13_screen cervicitis label). Not asked for: Step 2/3 management, doses, and detail no Step 1 source teaches.
- Conventions: bN is the 0-based index into "body" of fast/content/topics/<tid>.json (rp12-rp15 hold img rows that content/topics lacks, so gated indices there are 1-2 lower). Every "Replace" quotes text that occurs once in that block today. Rows that insert a new paragraph row (["p", "..."]) shift later indices, so locate blocks by the quoted text or apply insertions last. Length bands checked (STEP1_ONLY: paragraph 15-170 words, call 15-110, steps body 12-110, topic 400-2600): every block stays inside after its row.
- Kinds: MISSING (a Step 1 fact the lesson never teaches; the sentence to add), QUALIFIER (taught, but without a condition, number or mechanism that changes the answer; the corrected sentence), ERROR (wrong; the correct sentence). No ERROR was found in rp1-rp15: the earlier rounds removed them.
