# Step 1 rewrite coverage audit B: rp15, rp16, rp17, rp18, rp21, rp22, rp23, rp24, rp25, rp26, rp27, rp28, rp29

Auditor: fresh Claude, 2026-10-05. Not a phase verdict; no token. Nothing in content, engine, scope, ledger, gate or plan was edited.

Method
- Each rewrite (fast/content/topics/<tid>.json) was read in full against the long version (git show 5cb3043:content/topics/<tid>.json), sentence by sentence (full read of both plus a scripted nearest-sentence diff to catch meaning shifts), against the topic section of scope/STEP1-BLUEPRINT.md and the worker cut lists (fast/ws/S01, S02, S03, S06 audit notes) and fast/ws/R1/audit/R1-notes.md.
- Every figure placed in each topic was read: fast/content/figs/<key>.json (cap, teach) and every text element and the aria-label of fast/content/figs/<key>.svg. Images placed in the topics and the visual-guide cards were also read (no findings there).
- Glossary: engine-faithful scan of the built page fast/repro-endo-path.html (served with serve.py --root fast on port 8814; topic bodies and figure texts on the page verified identical to fast/content). For every topic the scan ran explicitGloss + bodyBlock exactly as the Learn view does, before and after answering the self-explanation, and listed every link. All 80 cross-topic links touching these 13 topics, in either direction, were judged against the sentence they sit in. The server was stopped afterwards.
- Medical accuracy judged against First Aid, Pathoma, Robbins, Costanzo and current guidance (ACOG 2024 Rh update, HHS perinatal HIV guidelines of January 2023, USPSTF 2024 breast, CDC 2021 STI; dates noted where a fix carries one).
- R1 items re-checked and NOT re-reported: rp26 body[8] Rh immune globulin sentence is correct against the ACOG 2024 clinical practice update; aliases tunica, basal cells, inhibin, ADH, atypical hyperplasia, capacity, portal system and unopposed no longer mislink in or from these topics.
- Practice-item dependencies were checked before any deletion is proposed: the 24/48-hour nitrate interval (rp22 body[21], figure rp22_erection) is keyed by rapid/rp22 items and the fetal MCA Doppler clause in rp18 body[8] is used by a practice stem, so both are left alone. The one figure label that a practice pointer highlights (rp26_framework) has its pointer update written into the fix.
- Block numbers are 0-based indices into "body" (body[i]). SVG line numbers are as of this audit; match by the quoted old text. Kinds: CUT-STEP1, ERROR, NON-STEP1, FLOW, GLOSS.
