Method

- Files read: fast/content/topics/rp16-rp30.json, fast/content/figs/<key>.json and .svg for every figure the bodies place, fast/content/images/<key>.json for every placed image, fast/content/glossary/rp16-rp30.json, fast/content/guides/rp16-rp30.json. The content was re-read after the 01:01 merge of commit 99ae96e (rp24 b30 and rp27 b27 changed); every row below quotes the files as they stand after it. The built fast/repro-endo-path.html was grep-checked and carries the same text; the page was not served.
- Already fixed and not re-reported: every row of .repro/coverage/step1-audit-A.md and -B.md, the P3.V run-2 findings (git 797e6b7) and run-3 findings (.repro/verify/P3-report.md), including the rp30 teratogens added in R4 (methylmercury, alkylating agents, iodine, fluoroquinolones). DUPLICATE: a fact that another rp topic already teaches is not requested again; each topic lists those under "Not requested here".
- Not asked for: Step 2/3 management, doses beyond the classic ones, and detail no Step 1 source teaches. Sources are cited by edition, chapter and entry (FA = First Aid for the USMLE Step 1 2025); page numbers are omitted because they could not be verified from here.
- Conventions: bN is the 0-based index into "body" of fast/content/topics/<tid>.json (img rows included). Indices are those of the file before any edit; the two "Insert a new paragraph row" rows (rp17 after b10, rp29 after b24) shift later indices by one, so apply them last or locate blocks by the quoted text. Every "Replace" quotes text that occurs exactly once in that block today; rows for the same block apply in the order listed, and "the row above" means the previous row of the same topic. A literal | inside a glossary link is written \| here; in the file it is a plain |. Every proposed edit was applied to an in-memory copy and checked against the §7.11 bands (paragraph 20-170 words, call 25-110, step body 12-110, at most 3 bold spans per string, table rows 2-14): all pass.
- Kinds: MISSING (a Step 1 fact the lesson never teaches; the sentence to add), QUALIFIER (taught, but without a condition, number or mechanism that changes the answer; the corrected sentence), ERROR (wrong; the correct sentence).
