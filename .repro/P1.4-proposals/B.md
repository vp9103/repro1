Row format: `| OBJ-ID | topics | terms | anchors | unanchored | note |`
- terms: the proposed 2-4 terms for the terms column (lowercase, `;`-separated; each >= 5 characters or containing a digit). A word-start stem such as `viriliz` or `sensitiz` is meant to match any longer word.
- anchors: every named part of the objective -> the term or blueprint key that anchors it. `BP[a, b]` lists blueprint key terms of a topic of the row (so they are checked separately, not copied into the terms); each cited key was checked against `scope/STEP1-BLUEPRINT.md` (items rated x excluded). A sibling row id means that row's terms carry the part.
- unanchored: `-`, or the part that no 4-term budget can anchor (it becomes an UNANCHORED audit row).
- note: `-`, or what changed and why. "drops" lists a current term that was GENERIC, a BP duplicate, a bare number or a stem that matches unrelated text.
