**Method.** For every term: does it hit a block that teaches the named part, or only an unrelated block (a heading, another disease, a drug used elsewhere, a figure label, a self-explanation question)? Where no sharper word exists the part is logged UNANCHORED. Terms whose first hit is the right block and whose other hits are the same part or are forced by another anchor were kept (checked and unchanged: HP-UTERUS-OVARY.5 polycystic, LABOR-DELIVERY.1 prostaglandin, PREG-REVIEW.7 prepregnancy, ENDO-TBL-DM.2 first-line).

Every new term appears in its row's anchors cell.
