## 1. TOPIC-MAP

- **Terms filled for all 326 lecture rows.** Each row has 2–4 terms. Every term is lowercase and at least 5 characters
  long or contains a digit (`p53`, `e6`, `cd4`, `12p`, `a1c`, `4.5`, `126`). Each term is a word that a correct
  explanation of that objective in the first-listed topic has to contain. Most reuse the blueprint's `{k: ...}`
  vocabulary for the same topic, so the objective terms and the blueprint terms push the author toward the same wording.
  No term is a bare generic word (anatomy, clinical, patient, most common); such words appear only inside a specific phrase (`anatomy scan`, `patient-centered`).
- **70 BiCEP rows added** after ENDO-TBL-DM.4, one per `BICEP-*` objective, and each has a reference line at the end of
  the file. The `section` column is `-` on every row. No row was deleted.
- **One planning-session assignment was changed** (at the orchestrator's request, after my first report):
  - MENOPAUSE.4 (osteoporosis pathophysiology): `en10,rp11` became `rp11,en10`. It is a BSE 638 menopause objective that
    is on the 10/9 final. en10 is in wave 2, which is built after the final, so with en10 accountable no topic would have
    had to teach it before the exam. rp11 teaches the cause, estrogen loss, and already has to cover bone loss through
    BP-rp11-04. en10 stays as the second topic and teaches the full bone-turnover picture later. The terms are unchanged
    (`rankl; osteoprotegerin; osteoclast; trabecular`). rp11 will contain them naturally when it explains that estrogen
    loss raises RANKL and lowers osteoprotegerin, so osteoclast activity rises and trabecular bone is lost first.
- **Two assignments were reviewed and kept:**
  - ANAT-PELVIS-LAB.13 (gross anatomy of the testis, epididymis, ductus deferens and prostate) stays with rp1 first. It
    is a pelvis-lab objective and half of it is pelvic viscera. The rp1 terms therefore include `testis` and `epididymis`.
  - PATHPHARM-MALE.8 (alpha-1 blockers, 5-ARIs, PDE5 inhibitors, SSRIs) stays with rp22 first. The rp22 terms name the BPH
    drugs too (`tamsulosin`, `finasteride`), so rp22 has to teach them, while rp21 covers them under its own blueprint items.
- **Term conventions.** Terms are matched as lowercase substrings of the rendered text, so:
  - Stems cover inflections: `atypi`, `koilocyt`, `thromboembol`, `contracepti`, `hypothalam`, `viriliz`.
  - Names with a diacritic or an apostrophe are given by their plain part: `paramesonephric` or `wolffian` for
    (Müllerian), `health initiative` for the Women's Health Initiative, `hippel` for von Hippel–Lindau, and
    `reductase deficiency` for 5α-/5-alpha-reductase deficiency.
  - Abbreviations of 4 letters or fewer are written out: `chorionic gonadotropin` for hCG, `pertussis` for Tdap,
    `gliflozin` for SGLT2 inhibitors, `ca-125`, `loop electrosurgical` for LEEP.
  - **No row uses `a|b`.** The row regex in `repro_common.topic_map_rows` (and `topic_map`) reads the terms cell as
    `([^|]*?)`, so a `|` inside the cell counts as a column break and the whole row fails to parse. `topicmap_ok` would
    then report "no TOPIC-MAP row". I tested this, and it is also noted in the file header. Every term is therefore a
    single spelling, and topic authors must use that exact form at least once.
